A Grand Super Cycle in stock prices got under way in the United States in 1857.
The first wave of this degree of movement ran from 1857 to 1928. The second
wave — representing a correction of the first wave — ran from November 1928
to 1932. The third wave in the Grand Super Cycle started in 1932 and has many
years to run.
The Grand Super Cycle wave from 1857 to 1928 is referred to as “No. 1”, but it
may have been No. 3 or No. 5.² A severe depression occurred from 1854 to 1857
similar in duration to that of 1929-1932.
