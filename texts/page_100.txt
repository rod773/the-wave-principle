Figure 37 shows a pattern where the end of the correction at “2” is higher than
the end of wave “A” of the correction, indicating unusual strength of the
subsequent movement. (The second correction shown in Figure 37 is weaker.)
Corrections of bear trends, that is, corrections following downward movements,
have the same characteristics as those of advancing movements, but in reverse
(see Figures 38 through 40).
