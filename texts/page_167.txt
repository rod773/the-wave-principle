The 1937-1938 Bear Market (see Figures 62 and 63) provided a number of
novelties, for example:
Parallelogram
August 4,1937, at 187.31, was the “orthodox” top of a rally.¹ Then followed a
dip of three waves and an advance of three waves to 190.38, August 14th.
Between these two dates waves A and B of an irregular correction were formed
(see Figure 64). Wave C was very rapid and long, down to 115.82 on October
18th, and formed a perfect parallelogram. It has no particular significance for
that reason. The speed and extent were spectacular, and indicated by the
“irregular” top, the same as that of 1928/1929/1932 (see Figure 65).
