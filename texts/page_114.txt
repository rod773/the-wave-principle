Triangles
Wave movements occasionally taper off to a point or broaden out from a point in
the form of a triangle. These triangular formations are important since they
indicate the direction the market will take at the conclusion or approximate apex
of the triangle.
Triangles are of two classes — horizontal and diagonal. Horizontal triangles
represent hesitation on the part of prices. At the conclusion of a horizontal
triangle the market will resume the same trend — upward or downward —
which it was pursuing previous to triangular hesitation. Horizontal triangles are
simply hesitations and have the same significance as flats. If a zig-zag appears as
wave 2, a flat or triangle will appear as wave 4 (see Figure 44).¹³ If a flat or
triangle occurs as wave 2, a zig-zag will appear as wave 4 (see Figure 45).¹⁴
