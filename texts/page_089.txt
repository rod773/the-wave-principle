8) Retracement means that the travel of a described movement between two
specified points is covered again. For example, a correction and resumption of
the trend is a double retracement.
If a trader is holding “longs” when a downward extension appears, he should not
sell then because the market will immediately retrace the extension in three
waves, before seeking lower levels.
