SPEED, VOLUME AND CHARTING¹
High speed by the market in one direction almost invariably produces a
corresponding high speed in the reversal, as for example, the midsummer 1932
advance covered forty points (Dow-Jones Industrial Average) or 100% in nine
weeks. This was equal to 4 1/2 points per week. Note the decreasing speed of
advances from 1932 to 1937 in Figure 52.
